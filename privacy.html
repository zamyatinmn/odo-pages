Privacy Policy for ODO

Last updated: September 25, 2026

ODO is an offline vehicle maintenance and expense tracking application developed by Maksim Zamyatin.

Data Collection

ODO does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

The application does not require user registration or an account and does not use analytics, advertising, tracking technologies, or third-party services that collect user data.

ODO does not have Internet access.

Data Storage

Information entered into ODO, such as vehicle information, mileage, fuel records, maintenance records, and expenses, is stored locally on the user's device.

This information is not transmitted to the developer or to third parties.

Permissions

ODO may request notification permission in order to display reminders and other application notifications.

This permission is used only for functionality provided directly on the user's device.

Children

ODO does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if ODO's functionality or data practices change. The current version will always be available on this page.
